How to prepare

  • • Read the job description and bring examples of work you are proud of.
  • • Be ready to explain your decisions, trade-offs, and how you collaborate.
  • • Prepare questions about the role, team, and the way we work.
  • • Tell us if you need an accessibility adjustment or a different interview format.

Our commitment to you

  • • You will always know which stage you are in.
  • • Interviews are scheduled for one hour and never intentionally overlapped.
  • • Your CV and personal information are kept private.
  • • AI may help recruiters summarise a CV, but people make every decision.
